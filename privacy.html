Privacy Policy

Poker Calculator: Odds & Outs · Effective November 6, 2026

Poker Calculator: Odds & Outs ("the app") is made by Jesse Ford. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it does not collect any.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

How the app works

All calculations happen on your device. The app does not connect to the internet and does not send the cards, bets or any other input you enter anywhere.

Information stored on your device

The app saves one setting on your device: whether you chose the Advanced (poker terms) wording. It stays on your device, is not shared, and is removed if you delete the app.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking tools, so no information is shared with third parties.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children. It is not a gambling app and offers no real-money play.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
